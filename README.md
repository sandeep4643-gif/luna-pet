# 🐱 Luna – Little Comfort Pet 💗

A little kitten, a little comfort, and a whole lot of happiness!

**Luna – Little Comfort Pet** is a cute, interactive virtual pet mini-game designed to bring moments of relaxation, comfort, and joy through playful interactions and calming activities.

Spend time with Luna, take a little break from your busy day, and enjoy some peaceful moments with your virtual companion.

## ✨ Features

- 🐾 **Interactive Pet:** Feed, play, cuddle, and put Luna to sleep.
- 🎬 **Animated Interactions:** Unique video animations for different activities.
- 🎵 **Soothing Audio:** Background music, meowing, and purring sound effects.
- 💗 **Pet Statistics:** Track Luna's happiness, hunger, comfort, and energy.
- 🌈 **Mood Check-in:** Choose how you're feeling and interact with Luna.
- 🌿 **Breathing Exercise:** Take a moment to relax and breathe.
- 💌 **Positive Affirmations:** Enjoy little reminders to stay positive.
- ⭐ **XP and Levels:** Earn experience points through interactions.

## 🛠️ Built With

- CSS3
